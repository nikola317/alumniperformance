About Alumni Performance

Our Mission

Our goal at Alumni Performance is to connect local alumni athletes to youth athletes who are aspiring to fill these coaches' shoes one day. We care about the youth athlete's individual journeys, and most of the times the journeys are similar to those of alumni - so if the youth athletes train with their alumni, they will play like their alumni (and better).

We want to make a change in the sporting system in the United States, as we are aware that many families in towns and cities are extremely underwhelmed by their local sports organizations. Through our platform, we're creating meaningful connections between proven champions and the next generation of athletes.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the three-way integration platform work?
Our platform connects three key components: Alumni Performance (central coordination), Schools (who verify and hire their alumni), and Young Athletes (who book sessions). We sync real-time data between school management systems, field permit databases, and our booking platform to ensure seamless coordination.
How does the meet-up system work for safety?
We're developing a mobile app that provides secure session codes. When a session is booked, the trainee receives a unique code linked to the booking time. At the start of the session, the youth athlete gives this code to the coach, officially marking the session start. At the end, a new code appears on the app to confirm session completion.
How are alumni trainers verified?
All alumni trainers are verified and hired directly by their former schools. This ensures authenticity and maintains the school's standards. Schools manage their own alumni network and can add or remove trainers as needed.
How do you ensure field availability?
We integrate directly with school and municipal field permit systems, providing real-time availability updates. Our system automatically checks both trainer availability and field permits before allowing bookings, eliminating scheduling conflicts.
What sports do you cover?
We cover all sports! Each school manages their own alumni network across different sports. Whether it's football, soccer, basketball, baseball, tennis, or any other sport, schools can add alumni trainers from any athletic program.
How do schools join the network?
Schools can request partnerships through our platform. We work with them to set up field permit integrations, verify alumni trainers, and establish their presence on our network. The process typically takes 2-4 weeks from application to go-live.
